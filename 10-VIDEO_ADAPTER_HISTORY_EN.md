# My video adapter implementation history

> Historical context: I preserve this complete record as a historical revision. Pending, not-yet-integrated, or untested statements below describe that revision at the time. I keep later qualification separate and do not retroactively broaden these results. The named local evidence artifacts are references, not attachments to this document.

- I first compiled the two video bridge TUs to AMD64 in `compile-attempt1`. That was compilation only, before the later queue/notification and reader-boundary repairs; it is preserved as historical evidence.
- The first facade harness encountered LNK1101 in the sandbox MSPDB handoff. It ended without executing the harness. An attempted process-identity guard refused to stop a process that no longer matched; no Stop-Process executed. Repeating the same harness with the authorized escalated native build produced a real executable and passed. I preserved both attempts.
- I corrected a notification publication race, contained caller/reader exceptions, made policy initialization failure-safe and tested bounded queues, cancellation and frame ownership. The first positive facade run had 115 checks; later versions added measured SDK1 negative conversions, lease exhaustion, asynchronous error retention and integer-boundary cases. Earlier results remain tied to their own source snapshots.
- I measured SDK1 MEMORYBUFFER alpha using isolated fresh frames. The initial alpha observation was inconclusive and was not used as an oracle. The later V3 measurement showed identical fresh RGB/BGRA with alpha 255 at all tested alpha settings; I corrected the memory-context conversion accordingly. D3D alpha remains a separate gate.
- My first real bridge observation passed SDK initialization, actual Verify, GPU upload/draw/readback, leases and Reset but failed terminal EOF after queued async reopen/seek/play. A direct real backend observation showed synchronous native Open→Seek→Play reached the final frames. I removed the redundant SDK-native asynchronous Open from the bridge worker. The same real harness then passed; I retained the failed observations and source snapshots.
- The four-AVI NULL-sound cases and one muted DirectSound logo case then passed 165 checks. No game or server was opened. The facade's fake-decoder matrix and the exact Reader/CNG boundary test are separate evidence; their counts are not presented as full SDK, GPU or game tests.
- SoundV1's 879 checks are historical. I corrected consumed-PCM loss during Pause and concurrent Open admission in SoundV2, with 901 checks and an independent review. The video bridge compiles and observes that exact V2 source. I did not rewrite the older receipts.
- I moved a pending header-comment clarification out of the active inputs while the real cases ran. Its parameter-name/comment-only change was not used to claim a new ABI; the compiled header snapshot and final current header bytes match. All source snapshots and raw native receipts remain available.

- In a separate review, I found a missing Borrow epoch check for a source callback admitting CloseAsync/StopAsync inside Frame. I preserved the current source, added checks after decode and before publication/lease, and reproduced two safe baseline failures versus 152/152 V2 checks. Previously returned leases remain valid.
- I then measured six actual SDK1 D3D9 alpha/format cases. All exposed X8 textures and unchanged RGB/BGRA at alpha0/128/255, even with A8 requested. I proceeded with matching that exposed contract. I preserved the V1 source and updated only the output-alpha/format policy; a real V1 A8 case failed the new measured format oracle, while six V2 native cases passed 210 checks. Internal compositing, screen presentation and game integration remain outside this evidence.
