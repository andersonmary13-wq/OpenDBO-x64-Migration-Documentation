# My implementation design for an SDK 1.1 facade over Bandi Video 2.1

I record this historical status as a **private design and declaration candidate only**. I have not compiled, linked or installed anything in this directory or produced a replacement DLL at this stage. I have not edited an existing Core file. My initial AMD64 Client build and its static qualification precede this adapter.

## How I define the binding and integration boundary

I plan to keep the existing SDK1 `IBandiVideo` facade for the two callers while implementing it with my own C++ code. I will not reinterpret an `IBandiVideo2*` as an `IBandiVideo*`, rename `bdvid64.dll` to `BVL64.dll` or pretend its export has the old factory signature. I retain the distinction that SDK1 `Release()` returns HRESULT and is not IUnknown, whereas SDK2 inherits real IUnknown with ULONG Release. I treat their enums, vtables, frame contracts and factories as different.

I plan to use two implementation translation units. I will include only the existing SDK1 header and a project-owned neutral contract in the facade TU. In the backend TU, I will include Windows/IUnknown/assert/tchar globally first, then the unmodified external SDK2 `bandivid.h` inside a dedicated namespace. I require that TU not to include SDK1. I recognize that a namespace alone does not isolate SDK preprocessor macros such as BVL_VERSION and BVERR codes. I will not use the sample CBandiVideoLibrary's dummy IUnknown implementations as a COM ownership model; I will call the factory into the actual typed IBandiVideo2 object and release that real object.

I plan to load the approved absolute `bdvid64.dll` path with `LoadLibraryExW` and `LOAD_LIBRARY_SEARCH_DLL_LOAD_DIR | LOAD_LIBRARY_SEARCH_SYSTEM32`. I will reject an unexpected basename, architecture, file pin or export. I will not use current-directory/PATH search, a global SetDllDirectory change, fallback to another unsigned DLL, a guessed export or a nonpublic entry point. I will keep an owned module reference until every SDK object, reader/sound callback and worker has stopped and released. I require multiple movies to share a module owner, never an unprotected borrowed factory pointer. On failure, I will release interfaces before unloading the module. I do not treat this design as authorization to redistribute; I retain the SDK license as a separate prerequisite.

I can use an internal, statically linked `CreateLegacyVideo21` function matching the old two-argument factory type for CNtlMovieManager. I plan for it to create my facade, which separately calls the real four-argument CreateBandiVideo2. I require CMoviePlayer's wrapper to use the same owned factory/module path under x64. I will keep Win32 and its original SDK untouched. I will never ship a compatibility export that forwards through coincidentally similar vtables.

## How I require verification and errors to behave

I require every supplied Verify call to forward to the actual SDK2 object with the supplied arguments and return its exact HRESULT. I will not log, copy to a fixture, serialize, substitute or manufacture successful credentials. I will not bypass verification or infer success from evaluation decoding. I will not silently recreate a verified SDK object and transfer an old object's authorization state to it.

I recognize that CNtlMovie::Create already checks Verify failure, while CMoviePlayer::ReadyMovie currently calls Verify and ignores its result. I require the eventual integration to check that real result before Open; preserving the call does not mean preserving an unchecked failure. I require the consumer to clean up on failure. I do not yet know whether the existing license is accepted by SDK2; my decoding probe deliberately did not call Verify.

I will return the real failing operation's HRESULT wherever the facade method permits it. I will preserve SDK2 S_FALSE rather than turn every nonfailure into fabricated S_OK. I require a failed Open/Play/Seek, frame upload or notification not to advance the acknowledged state. I have no E_NOTIMPL or fake-success feature placeholders in the candidate declaration. I regard the missing contracts below as blockers to claiming a complete adapter.

## How I plan command ordering, state and asynchronous operations

I plan one decoder-control owner and a bounded FIFO. I plan OpenAsync to use SDK2 Open(path, TRUE); SDK2 has no PlayAsync/SeekAsync/StopAsync/CloseAsync. I therefore require real queued commands with copied paths/arguments and observable completion/errors for those legacy methods, not synchronous methods renamed Async. I require synchronous methods to wait for their own command result without holding engine, GUI, D3D, module or sound-callback locks. I require a full queue to return the legacy queue-full failure without changing state. I require the decoder worker not to touch HWND or D3D or call gameplay callbacks.

I will serialize close/seek/play/queries/frame decode with an opening-completion barrier. I observe that CNtlMovie::DeviceRestoreCallback currently submits OpenAsync, SeekAsync and PlayAsync in that exact order; I will retain it and not submit Seek/Play while status is OPENING. I require every accepted open/close/stop/new playback to invalidate earlier command/notification generations. I will capture arguments and an explicit generation, never pointers to stack commands or transient path strings. I require Release to drain or cancel commands, stop the SDK and sound callbacks, then join the worker before destroying state or unloading the DLL. I will test shutdown from every partial-create/open/error state.

I will map SDK2 CLOSED to SDK1 CREATED/STANDBY only when my facade has been created; otherwise I will use UNINITIALIZED. I will map READY to OPENED and map PLAYING and PAUSED explicitly; I treat OPENING as pending, not a fabricated successful open. I will use SDK1 SEEKING as bridge state while a real seek is pending. SDK2 ERROR and PLAYEND have no identical SDK1 enum, so I will retain the actual failure/EOF state separately. I will not static_cast status or pixel enums between SDKs. I retain SDK1 X8R8G8B8=0 and A8R8G8B8=1 as distinct from SDK2, which begins with NULL=0 and A8R8G8B8=5.

## How I plan the source reader and sound

I can use SDK2's default reader for paths; I will convert LPCSTR with the old ANSI API's Windows ACP semantics and reject failed conversion. For Open(IBandiVideoSource*), I will provide a real SDK2 IBandiVideoFileReader bridge: Open binds the current source, Read forwards, SetPosition/GetPosition forward and GetFileSize maps GetLength. I recognize that the old source is not IUnknown. I will not invent AddRef or delete it; I require its caller to retain it through pending asynchronous commands until Close completes. I will give the wrapper itself correct IUnknown ownership. Because the factory binds a reader at object creation, I will use a switchable reader bound at creation for path/source changes, not recreate the SDK after Verify or pass a dangling source. I will not let reopening a packed source resolve an arbitrary disk path.

I require real implementations of both audio modes. I plan a switchable IBandiVideoSound bound at factory creation to support DSOUND playback and a silent clock/PCM sink for BVL_AUDIO_DEVICE_NULL. I will not treat an SDK-default sound chosen once as silently implementing both later per-Open modes. I will reuse documented sample DirectSound/Null contracts after pinning their sources, or retain the SDK default only if every requested mode is explicitly implemented. I require an advancing playback clock even for NULL sound, because returning constant zero can stall video. I will preserve pause, stop, seek, volume 0..255 and PCM channel/sample byte counts. I require audio callbacks not to outlive their instance or deadlock with Close/worker join. I will not call or reuse the game's FMOD system simply to bypass this missing layer.

## How I plan frame transport and D3D9 ownership

I recognize that SDK2 GetFrame takes a **caller-owned CPU buffer**, confirmed by my real decode and sample observation, and has no ReleaseFrame lease. I will allocate that buffer in the bridge and populate frame_buf, frame_buf_size, pitch, dimensions and requested pixel format before GetFrame. I will explicitly check positive dimensions, supported format, row bytes, pitch, height, INT frame-size and SIZE_T multiplication bounds. I will respect the documented sample's row pitch and signed orientation, never assume contiguous width*height*4 storage for every format. I retain SDK2 frame_time as 100 ns and frame_number as a 64-bit frame index. I will keep output storage alive across the decode command.

I require uploads to run only on the render thread. I plan a SYSTEMMEM staging texture and a compatible DEFAULT-pool shader-readable destination, copying validated rows under LockRect, unlocking and then calling UpdateTexture. I will create no new device, window, Present or BeginScene inside the adapter. I will not combine D3DUSAGE_RENDERTARGET with an assumption of dynamic CPU locking. I will keep X8/A8/RGB565 conversion explicit and preserve colors/orientation. I treat alpha as a separate bridge operation because SDK2 has no SetAlpha.

I plan CheckFrame to poll readiness without consuming a frame. I plan GetLastFrame to decode/upload at most one selected frame and return the borrowed IDirect3DTexture9 expected by both callers. I will retain the destination reference in the bridge and track every borrow; I require ReleaseLastFrame to validate the pointer and return that borrow once. I will not overwrite a borrowed texture: I will use a bounded pool or reject/wait until its lease returns. I require GetLastFrame(void*) and GetLastFrame(void**) to share the same contract, including null on failure. I require a real IBVLMemoryBuffer/HBITMAP wrapper for MEMORYBUFFER, not a cast from a texture pointer. Although the two actual game consumers request DIRECTX9, I will not hide a legacy feature loss by claiming complete memory-buffer support without implementing it.

I require new uploads to stop before device reset, in-flight upload to finish/cancel and staging/default textures to release after every borrow returns, retaining playback position/pause/repeat. I will restore on the render thread after the existing device becomes usable. I require Close/reopen/seek/play to obey the queued generation barrier. I will preserve CMoviePlayer's intentional stop on device release instead of automatically restarting it, and CNtlMovie's intentional reopen/seek/play behavior. I require device errors to return errors, never a successful empty frame.

I identified an existing CNtlMovie::UpdateRenderTexture GetSurfaceLevel-failure path that acquires a frame without calling ReleaseLastFrame. I require eventual integration to use a scoped borrower covering every return. I also observed that CNtlMovie::Delete does not directly release its owned m_pRenderTexture. I will not conceal those consumer lifetimes by forcibly releasing caller-held references or changing global D3D ownership.

## How I plan repeat, timing, alpha and EOF

I recognize that SDK2 has no repeat argument and will retain requested repeat mode in the facade. At PLAYEND I require repeating playback to perform real Seek(0, TIME) followed by Play once, respecting errors, without EOF for a successful loop. I require nonrepeating playback to latch EOF only after handling the final available frame. I will not let repeated CheckFrame/GetVideoInfo/IsPlaying calls produce duplicate terminal notifications.

I will use checked seconds-to/from-100 ns conversions with finite/nonnegative/INT64-range checks and rational fps_rate/fps_scale conversions, never cast seconds directly to SDK2 ticks. I will derive CurrentRenderTime from the frame actually delivered. I require CurrentPlayTime to use the sound or silent playback clock plus seek/pause state, not merely the last frame timestamp. SDK2 video info has no ErrorFrames count; I will expose a bridge decode-failure count only with an explicitly documented semantic decision, not call it an SDK counter. I require tests to determine FramesToTime and TimeToFrames rounding/negative/overflow behavior before implementing their no-HRESULT legacy signatures.

I recognize that SDK2 has no SetAlpha equivalent. I will store validated alpha 0..255 and implement/test conversion or composition in bridge output, including per-pixel A8 and RGB565. I prohibit returning success while ignoring alpha. The supplied headers do not establish whether legacy SetAlpha replaces or multiplies existing alpha; I will retain that contract as unresolved until a sample/documented rule or an explicitly scoped compatibility decision is approved.

I recognize that SDK2 has no HWND EOF/opened notification. I require my adapter to pump actual status and post the registered legacy message on the UI thread with a generation cookie. I observe that CMoviePlayer::HandleEvent currently accepts any WM_BVNOTIFY_EOF for its HWND; I require it to validate the cookie against current playback so old queued EOF cannot stop a new movie. I will publish at most once per generation after successful PostMessage, without invoking user callbacks inside codec/control locks. I require Stop/cancel/focus-loss to invalidate pending EOF before callbacks, with caller Stop remaining the single completion-callback owner. I will treat WM_BVNOTIFY_OPENED similarly if a caller uses it. I use exactly-once here to mean successful delivery/consumption for one generation, not a claim that Windows cannot drop messages after window destruction.

## How I require qualification before calling integration complete

1. I will compile the official SDK2 header in its isolated TU and the unchanged SDK1 declarations in the facade TU and verify factory/IUnknown calls against the real SDK, never fake vtables.
2. I will test pure contracts: conversion boundaries, enum maps, FIFO/open barriers, full queue, command failures, stale generation, repeat/EOF once, Stop/Close/Release idempotence, partial-create failure and callback reentrancy.
3. I will test real SDK decoding with the existing private intro AVI files: caller-owned buffer/pitch/format/hash, exact frame sequence/EOF, pause/seek/repeat, path/source reader, sound and silent-clock modes. I require no credential reuse in probes and retain Verify as its own authorized, result-bearing test.
4. I will perform real offscreen D3D9/device qualification only under new authorization: upload/hash/color/alpha, outstanding texture leases, loss/reset, both callers and no leaked COM/module references or post-Close callbacks. I do not treat a CPU decode pass as proof of this layer.
5. I require a full Client rebuild/link/static check with both consumers and a human movie test while preserving the installed Win32 baseline. I keep licensing/redistribution approval separate from native correctness.

Until I implement and qualify those contracts and missing equivalents, I retain this as a design, not a working codec adapter or stable Client64 release.
