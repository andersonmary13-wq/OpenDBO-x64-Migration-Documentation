# My private video64 investigation and native observations

> Historical context: I preserve this complete record as a historical revision. Pending, not-yet-integrated, or untested statements below describe that revision at the time. I keep later qualification separate and do not retroactively broaden these results. The named local evidence artifacts are references, not attachments to this document.

I kept this work outside the original Core, SDK32, running client, operating-system codec registration and Git. I did not run a game, GPU device, physical audio device, vendor installer or sample executable. My sources, command files, failed results and later results remain in separate versioned paths.

1. I inspected the existing movie consumers and four local AVI files without changing them. I identified caller contracts beyond decoding: texture ownership, ready/play/stop, callbacks, seek/pause/repeat, render-target restoration and device loss. My read-only feasibility record is `../migracao64-dependencias/video64-native-backend-feasibility-readonly-v1.json`. I did not equate CPU decoding with those product contracts.
2. I compiled an AMD64 DirectShow probe with real Windows AVI splitter, MPEG decoders, SampleGrabbers and NullRenderers. I connected only the explicitly chosen graph, with no IntelligentConnect/external codec fallback. `compile-20261019T161500Z-e43a85c4` compiled without warnings; `run-20261019T161512Z-2cb93f9c` retained four negotiation failures, `0x80040207`. I did not claim EOF or decoded hashes from those failures.
3. I prepared a second DirectShow probe using the actual MPEG sequence descriptor in each AVI. I required both endpoint `QueryAccept` calls to accept the exact type before connecting. `compile-v2-20261019T161918Z-20c40d22` compiled; `run-v2-20261019T161927Z-4f456865` showed decoder acceptance but splitter rejection (`S_FALSE`). I preserved the failure instead of forcing a contradictory media type or installing a codec.
4. After I obtained the SDK2 repository and inspected its signature and declarations, I ran only the specifically authorized signed SDK2 evaluation DLL. I pinned its SHA256 and actual header. I initially did not call `Verify` and supplied a custom headless sound instrument plus caller-owned R8G8B8 CPU buffer. `sdk2-compile-20261019T162541Z-49f26ac1` compiled, and `sdk2-run-20261019T162554Z-93d68b07` traversed all four EOF states and expected frame indices. The initial success label was insufficient: I later established that every video buffer was zero. I did not change that historical receipt.
5. I added PCM nonzero counters and exactly three lossless frame observations per AVI in a new variant. `sdk2-compile-v2-20261019T163151Z-5dc10080` compiled, and `sdk2-run-v2-20261019T163159Z-32238246` retained twelve black PNGs. I independently computed hashes of repeated zero frames and matched all four aggregate video hashes. PCM contained nonzero samples, but pulled counts exceeded or undershot declared AVI audio duration. I explicitly withdrew any implication of useful visual decoding, complete source-audio counts or AV sync. I wrote `sdk2-video-output-corrective-addendum-v1.json` to qualify the old labels without overwriting them.
6. I proceeded to test the exact current product `Verify` arguments, unchanged. I read them only in memory and sent them through anonymous stdin; I did not compile, print, save, hash or put them in argv/environment. My first Verify probe compile failed because I omitted `<string>` for `std::getline`; I preserved `sdk2-verify-compile-20261019T163513Z-00b4ef23` and the source. My separate corrected source compiled in `sdk2-verify-compile-v2-20261019T163541Z-7bc5e3dd`. The actual SDK rejected empty and dummy arguments with `E_FAIL (0x80004005)` and accepted the unchanged existing product arguments with `S_OK`. `sdk2-verify-run-v2-20261019T163551Z-ba8d769b` records only numeric results. I did not interpret API success as proof of legal entitlement or redistribution rights. Bounded polling observed no visible child window or network endpoint; I did not claim that polling proves absence of all transient activity.
7. I repeated full decoding with real successful `Verify` but unchanged R8G8B8 output in `sdk2-compile-v3-20261019T163744Z-ccb1479e` / `sdk2-run-v3-20261019T163801Z-746fc352`. All four files still produced zero video pixels. I retained those failures and did not attribute the cause automatically to licensing or missing codecs.
8. I compared the vendor's first documented D3D9 format, X8R8G8B8, in a separate probe. I changed buffer size/pitch/format to four bytes per pixel and excluded the unused X byte from nonzero RGB counters. I retained `can_skip=FALSE`, real `Verify`, the same DLL and unchanged AVI hashes. `sdk2-compile-v4-20261019T163948Z-52f206fd` compiled; `sdk2-run-v4-20261019T164028Z-a9998d0d` reached EOF for 550 / 4580 / 4585 / 7042 frames. The nonzero RGB frame counts were 548 / 4580 / 4585 / 6987. I checked native per-frame hashes, full index coverage and monotonic timestamps. I opened all twelve first/middle/last PNGs: middle images were coherent logos/animation, and endpoint samples showed black/nearly black fades. I did not retouch them or remove a watermark. `sdk2-x8-native-observation-qualification-v1.json` and `sdk2-x8-visual-qa-v1.json` record these limited observations.
9. I materialized only the needed vendor reference texts under `sdk2-reference`, normalized to LF. I clearly marked them as normalized texts rather than raw-byte-identical downloads. My CustomPlayback excerpt excludes the registration example. I kept adapter/product integration separate from this investigation; I retained the successful X8 buffer contract and failed R8 evidence for that work.

I still have not qualified physical sound, audio fidelity, full source PCM duration, AV sync, GPU presentation, device loss, callbacks, pause/seek/repeat, product integration, full feature parity or legal licensing. No operating-system codec was installed or registered. Future community publication remains conditional on satisfactory human gameplay testing; I have not published these private vendor-derived materials.
